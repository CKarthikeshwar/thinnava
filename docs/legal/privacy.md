# Thinnava?! Privacy Policy

_Last updated: DRAFT, not yet published. Contact: <CONTACT EMAIL>_

Thinnava?! ("Thinnava", "we") is a student project built at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc). It helps you remember where you and the people you know have eaten. This policy explains what we collect and why, in plain words.

## What we collect
- **From your Google account, when you sign in:** your name, email address and profile photo. We use them to create your account. We never see your Google password.
- **What you create in the app:** your profile (name, username, bio), your visits and reviews, the places on your wishlist, what friends told you that you saved, internet finds and their links, your connections, your lists, and who you tagged.
- **Your location:** only when you allow it, and only while you use the app, to centre the map and show nearby places first. We don't store your location.
- **A notification token:** if you turn on notifications, so we can send you requests and tags.

## Who can see what
- People you're **not connected** with see only your name, username and photo.
- **Connections** see the visits and wishlist you share with them, based on the lists you choose ("All connections", "Only me", or your own lists).
- What you log about a friend's experience, and internet finds, are **only ever visible to you**.

## Shared messages and AI
When you share a message or link into Thinnava, its text is sent to **Google's Gemini** service to pick out the place, dishes and price. You check everything before saving. **Thinnava does not store the original message.** For internet finds, the link you shared is saved with your entry. Google may process the text under its own terms.

## Place information
Place names, addresses and locations come from **Google Maps**. Google's ratings and opening hours are only fetched when you ask for them, and aren't stored by us.

## What we don't do
No ads. We don't sell or share your data with anyone for marketing. No tracking or analytics.

## Where data is stored
Your data is stored with **Supabase**, our database provider, in its Mumbai (India) region.

## Deleting your data
You can delete your account at any time from **Settings → Delete account**. This permanently deletes everything you created. Your name is removed from other people's tags.

## Changes
If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
