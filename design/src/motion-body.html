Motion

Fast and quiet for everyday navigation; one authored moment where it matters: a friend's message becoming a saved entry. Tap any Play, or use the phone directly. Every pattern is a Material 3 transition, themed with Thinnava's own curves and the chef cat.

Signature

From WhatsApp to saved≈ 3.5 s end to end · emphasized curves
  1. The shared bubble lights up and lifts; the Thinnava sheet rises from the bottom (400 ms).
  2. The chef cat climbs up behind the card and hooks its paws over the edge (500 ms).
  3. A copy of the message flies into the reading card while the shimmer runs.
  4. The sheet grows into the confirmation screen; fields fill in one by one (40 ms stagger), the price flag pulses once.
  5. Typing a friend's name wakes the Save button.
  6. Save shrinks the screen into a new card that drops into the top of Home; the other cards slide down to make room.
Launch → welcomeCat lands 500 ms · fade through 300 ms

The cat settles in, the wordmark follows 100 ms later, then splash fades through to slide 1.

Peeking cat500 ms · emphasized decelerate

On Set up profile (and every peeking-cat screen) the cat climbs from behind the card, then its paws come over the edge. The username check spins, then the tick pops in.

Navigation

Welcome slidesShared axis X · 300 ms

Slides move sideways together; the active dot stretches into a pill.

Switching tabsSoft crossfade · 280 ms

The new tab settles in on top (from 98% scale) while the old one fades beneath it, so there is never a blank frame. The bar itself never moves, only its pill indicator.

Card → place pageTitle glide 460 ms · content rise 320 ms, 45 ms stagger · back 380 ms

The place name lifts off the card and glides up to become the page title; the card dims, the page settles in, and the buttons and people rise into place. Back fades the content and glides the title down into its card.

Bottom sheetsSlide up 400 ms · down 250 ms

Filter, pickers and the map pin card rise from below over a fading scrim; they leave faster than they arrive.

The + buttonFan out 250 ms · 30 ms stagger

Three circles spring out of the + along an arc; + turns into ×. Closing pulls them back in.

Map pinPin 150 ms · card 400 ms

The tapped pin grows a ring, then its card slides up from below.

Feedback

Wishlist heartPop 200 ms · snackbar 300 ms, stays 3 s

The heart fills and pops once; a snackbar confirms with a shortcut.
