Place page and entry detail

The place page leads with people, not ratings: your own history, then everyone you know who's been, each with what they loved, didn't like and paid. The hand-off buttons (Directions, Swiggy) sit right under the name. Google's rating, hours and price load only on a tap, so we never pay for them.

Copy or share in: from a message to saved

The hero flow. Copy a friend's message (WhatsApp only lets you copy) and tap Use copied text?, or share text and links from an app that allows it. Wait a second while the cat reads it, check what was found, add who told you, save. Every field can be fixed by hand, and the friend's name is never guessed.

Logging by hand

The same fields as the confirmation sheet, so there's one form to learn. Smart defaults (today, nearby places, All connections) mean most visits take a few taps.

Pickers and confirmations

Small sheets that slide up from below, each doing one job. They keep the forms short.

People, connections and lists

Connections are mutual and request-based, and you see nothing about someone until you're connected. "Their map" means a map of the places they've been and wishlisted, limited to what they've shared with you.

Notifications, settings and housekeeping

Settings follows your account reference: grouped white panels with a round icon, a label and a chevron. Housekeeping screens are plain lists.

Permission explainers

Asked only when a feature first needs them, never during onboarding. "Not now" never blocks the app.
