Thinnava?! Phase 1 UI · identity & main tabs

Splash and welcome

The chef cat is the brand. Onboarding is three screens: A1 Splash, A1b Intro (the promise and the social map in one screen), then A2 Welcome with Continue with Google. There is no "how to share" step: copying a friend's message is taught later, when it is useful.

Home

A single timeline, newest first, one card per entry. Every card leads with who and where, then what they loved, what they didn't and the price. The filter icon opens a sheet; the active filter shows as a chip so you always know what you're looking at.

Map

Pins are small pills showing who (a face) or what (heart = wishlist, tick = you went). Tapping a pin slides a card up from below. Layer chips sit on top so you can switch views in one tap.

Search

One search box, two segments: Places and People. Filter chips narrow the results. Places show who you know has been there.

My profile

Built like the Beli and account references: a centred identity block, quick counts, then your food life in three tabs. Settings and notifications sit in the top bar as round buttons (reference 4).

Palettes

Use the switches at the top. Sky is your chosen blue; Cobalt is brighter and punchier, Cerulean leans ocean blue-teal, Indigo is deeper with a hint of purple, and Denim is a muted grey-blue that matches the cat's fur. Review colours (Good green, Okayish amber, Bad red) stay the same in every palette.
