How it works

Three things, each takes seconds

The app is a diary shared only with people you know. Nothing else gets in the way.

1

Save what friends tell you

Copy their WhatsApp message and tap "Read it". We fill in the place; you just confirm.

2

Log where you ate

A rating, what was good, what wasn't. Done before the bill arrives.

3

See where your people went

Only people you've connected with. On a list or on a map, then jump to Maps or Swiggy.

Private by design

Privacy rules live in the database, not just the app. Friends see only what you share with them.

Friends, not strangers

No public reviews or star scores. Just what people you trust actually thought.

Free, no ads

An IISc project made in Bengaluru. No card, no tracking, no catch.
